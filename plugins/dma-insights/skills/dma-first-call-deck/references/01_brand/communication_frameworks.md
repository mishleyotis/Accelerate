## Framework 1: The Consultant's Approach

**Persuade through Logic.** Best for executive proposals, QBRs, DMA deliverables.

### The Pyramid Principle (Barbara Minto, McKinsey)
Lead with conclusion, support with evidence. Decision-makers form reactions within 60 seconds.

### SCQA Framework (Opening Narrative)

| Step | Purpose | DMA Example |
|---|---|---|
| **S** — Situation | Status quo everyone agrees on | "Your CU has grown 40% in membership and serves 120K members across 15 branches." |
| **C** — Complication | The disruption or problem | "However, onboarding still requires 3 visits and 12 days, causing 28% abandonment." |
| **Q** — Question | The implicit challenge | "How do you close these gaps to match or exceed peers?" |
| **A** — Answer | Your recommendation | "A phased 16-week roadmap starting with Digital Account Opening reduces onboarding to 10 minutes." |

### MECE Principle (Body Content)
Every set of supporting points must be:
- **Mutually Exclusive**: No two points overlap — if you can merge them, they overlap
- **Collectively Exhaustive**: Together they fully support the headline — if a skeptic can point to a missing factor, there's a gap

### Slide Structure
```
Slide 1: Title — Big Idea as headline
Slide 2: SCQA opening (or embedded in subtitle)
Slide 3: Executive summary — conclusion first, 3 supporting pillars
Slides 4–N: Evidence — one MECE argument per slide
Slide N+1: Recommendations — specific, actionable, time-bound
Slide N+2: Next steps — who does what by when
```

## Framework 2: The Visual Storyteller

**Persuade through Emotion.** Best for vision workshops, keynotes, culture transformation.

### The Sparkline Structure (Nancy Duarte)
Oscillate between "What Is" (painful reality) and "What Could Be" (better future).

| Beat | Purpose | Direction |
|---|---|---|
| 1. What Is — Opening | Current state and its pain | Down (tension) |
| 2. What Could Be | Better future, vivid and specific | Up (hope) |
| 3. What Is — Deepening | Obstacles and fears | Down (empathy) |
| 4. What Could Be — Resolution | Solution overcomes barriers | Up (confidence) |
| 5. Transformed Future | Inspiring vision of success | Peak (inspiration) |

### Visual Priority
- 10% recall for text-only vs 65% with visuals
- Headline + 1 supporting sentence max per slide
- Full-bleed images, before/after comparisons, data visualization
- Let imagery carry emotional weight

### Slide Structure
```
Slide 1: What Is — painful current reality (data-grounded)
Slide 2: What Could Be — better future (specific)
Slide 3: What Is — barriers (honest)
Slide 4: What Could Be — solution overcomes barriers (proof)
Slide 5: Transformed Future — inspiring close
```

## Framework 3: The Sales Narrative

**Persuade through Momentum.** Best for sales pitches, deal-closing, first meetings.

### AIDA Framework

| Phase | Purpose | Deck weight | Tactics |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Attention** | Hook immediately | ~10% | Startling stat, provocative question, bold claim |
| **Interest** | Explain THEIR problem | ~45% | Their data, their pain, their competitive landscape |
| **Desire** | Better future state | ~35% | ROI projections, case studies, demos, social proof |
| **Action** | Specific CTA | ~10% | "We propose a 2-week diagnostic. Can we schedule for March 10?" |

### The Mobilization Close
Never end with "Questions?" End with a specific action table:

| Date | Action Item | Owner |
|---|---|---|
| Specific date | Specific action | Named person |

Plus a goal statement: "Approval by Friday ensures we hit Q1 launch."

---

## The 10/20/30 Rule (Guy Kawasaki)

- **10 slides**: Max concepts a person absorbs. DMA gets 12 (data density ceiling).
- **20 minutes**: Even with a 60-min slot. Leave 40 for discussion.
- **30-point font**: Forces conciseness. Guideline: oldest audience age ÷ 2.

## Operational Blending Examples

**Blend 1: 70/30 Consultant + Storyteller (QBR / Renewal)**
Primary (Consultant) drives structure:
- Slides 1-2: Title + SCQA opening (data-driven)
- Slides 3-6: Evidence, scores, gaps (MECE structured)
- Slide 7: Recommendation (specific, actionable)
Secondary (Storyteller) adds emotional beats:
- Slide 2: Add "What Could Be" vision after the Complication
- Slide 6: Before/after visual showing transformation potential
- Slide 8: Close with inspired vision of next chapter

Worked headline stack showing the blend (8-10 headlines for a fictional QBR):
1. "Your platform served 45K transactions last quarter, 12% above forecast"
2. "Yet churn accelerated to 3.2% — below peers' 2.1% — signaling shifting member expectations"
3. "Three capabilities—Real-Time Settlement, Embedded Finance, and Fraud Analytics—close 65% of the experience gap"
4. "Across Operations, Data & Tech, and Member Experience, you trail industry peers by 0.4 on average maturity"
5. "Your strongest pillar, Governance, scores 3.7/5; Operations lags at 2.8/5"
6. "A 24-week roadmap repositions you from 3.1 to 3.6 maturity, matching top-quartile performance"
7. "Prioritizing Real-Time Settlement first unlocks $1.2M in annual cost savings and improves member experience by 34%"
8. "By Q4, you'll lead your peer set in digital adoption, creating a durable competitive moat"

**Blend 2: 80/20 Sales + Consultant (First Meeting / Intro)**
Primary (Sales/AIDA) drives structure:
- Slides 1-2: Attention hook + Interest (their problem)
- Slides 3-7: Desire (proof: case studies, stats, social proof)
- Slide 8: Action (specific CTA)
Secondary (Consultant) adds:
- Slide 4: One MECE evidence slide (logical proof point amid emotional narrative)
- Slide 7: Data-grounded ROI claim before the CTA

Worked headline stack for a fictional first meeting pitch (8-10 headlines):
1. "Three in five credit unions report member onboarding as their top digital blocker"
2. "Your member acquisition cost has climbed 18% YoY while conversion rates dropped from 61% to 54%"
3. "We worked with a similar $800M AUM CU that redesigned onboarding around mobile-first, reducing abandonment from 31% to 8%"
4. "Their roadmap prioritized three capabilities in sequence: Digital Account Opening → E-Signature → Real-Time Verification"
5. "The results: 2.4M new accounts opened digitally in year one, 35% increase in member satisfaction scores"
6. "Our diagnostic takes 4 weeks and costs $28K; the engagement typically generates $2.1M in unrealized efficiency gains across operations"
7. "We propose a 4-week Digital Onboarding Assessment starting March 15, with recommendations by April 12"
8. "Approval this week positions you to launch pilot improvements by Q2, capturing spring member acquisition window"

**Blend 3: 50/50 Storyteller + Consultant (Discovery Workshop)**
This is the most complex blend — alternating frameworks:
- Slide 1: What Is (Storyteller) — current pain
- Slide 2: What Could Be (Storyteller) — vision
- Slides 3-5: MECE Evidence (Consultant) — structured proof
- Slide 6: What Is — deeper barriers (Storyteller)
- Slides 7-8: Resolution (Consultant) — how to overcome barriers
- Slide 9: Transformed Future (Storyteller) — inspiring close

Worked headline stack for a fictional discovery workshop (9-10 headlines):
1. "Your member onboarding experience requires 3 visits and 12 days—20X slower than peer digital offerings"
2. "Imagine members opening an account in 10 minutes from their phone, any time, any place"
3. "Today, this gap costs you: 28% abandonment on mobile, 18% slower account funding, and $3.2M in annual administrative overhead"
4. "Your peers who moved to digital onboarding first saw 45% faster time-to-funded-account and 31% improvement in member satisfaction"
5. "Your biggest barriers: legacy underwriting systems (3-day manual review), fragmented identity verification, and paper-based document collection"
6. "Each barrier is solvable—but only if addressed in sequence, with the right technology partners and internal process redesign"
7. "A phased 18-week roadmap starting with Digital Document Collection unblocks the other capabilities and delivers $800K in first-year efficiency gains"
8. "Phase 1 (Weeks 1-6): Implement digital document upload and archive; reduce manual file handling by 60%"
9. "In one year, your members open accounts digitally in under 8 minutes, abandonment falls to 5%, and you've captured $2.1M in operational savings—positioning you as the digital leader in your peer set"

### When to Blend vs. Stay Pure
- Use pure framework when: audience is homogeneous, goal is singular, deck is <8 slides
- Blend when: audience is mixed (e.g., CEO + CTO), goal is dual (inform + persuade), deck is 10+ slides
- Never blend 3 frameworks — always pick a primary and one secondary

### Framework → Headline Tone Mapping
Table showing how each framework dictates headline voice:

| Framework | Tone | Pattern | Verb Style | Example |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Consultant | Logical, data-first | "[Actor] [status] [relative to benchmark]" | Shows, trails, leads | "Acme CU scores 2.4/5, trailing peers by 0.6 across all four pillars" |
| Storyteller | Emotional, aspirational | "[Current state] → [Future state]" | Transforms, enables, unlocks | "From manual underwriting to instant digital decisions—unlocking 40% faster account funding" |
| Sales | Urgent, impact-driven | "[Action] [because] [impact]" | Closes, captures, accelerates | "Three quick wins capture $1.2M in operational savings by Q3" |
