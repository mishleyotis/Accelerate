## 1. Brand Identity & Voice

Zennify is a data, AI, and customer experience consultancy for financial services. The brand is a trusted guide — not the hero. The client is the protagonist.

| Attribute | Do | Don't |
|---|---|---|
| Confident | "Your data governance gap represents a $2.1M opportunity." | "We believe this might possibly help." |
| Specific | "23% reduction in processing time." | "Significant improvement in certain areas." |
| Active voice | "We assessed 17 capabilities." | "The assessment was conducted by our team." |
| Second person | "Your team can achieve 3.2 maturity." | "The institution's team may see improvements." |
| Forward-looking | "Performance declined 12%, creating a $3.2M recapture opportunity." | "Performance declined." (no implication) |

## 2. Opportunity Framing (Critical for First Sales Call)

Every word must build trust and open doors. Maturity gaps are investment opportunities.

| NEVER use | ALWAYS use instead |
|---|---|
| Gap, deficit, weakness | Opportunity, area of focus, room for growth |
| Fails, lacking, poor | Has not yet established, early-stage |
| Trails, lags, falls behind | Positioned to accelerate toward, ready for next level |
| Below benchmark, low maturity | Highest-value transformation opportunity |
| Problem, issue, risk, threat | Priority investment area, area of focus |
| Immature | Activating, building |

---

## 3. Reframing Boundaries

Opportunity framing must NOT lead to inventing claims.

| ALLOWED (same semantic content) | PROHIBITED (adds meaning) |
|---|---|
| Directional claims: "trailing" → "positioned to close the gap" | Emotional adjectives absent from source: "dangerously," "critically" |
| Calculated deltas: "0.6 gap" → "0.6 points of upside" | Invented consequences: "causing market loss" unless source quantifies |
| Trend inference: "3x acceleration → time pressure" | Moral judgment: "must act now" unless source recommends |
| Benchmark comparisons | Extrapolated projections not in source data |

**Test:** Can you defend the statement by pointing to a specific source sentence? Yes = allowed. No = fabrication.

## 5. Typography

- **Font:** DM Sans only. Bold headings: DM Sans Medium. No serif. No Calibri/Arial/Noto Sans.
- **Case:** Sentence case everywhere. Never Title Case. Exception: EYEBROW subtitles in ALL CAPS ("THE ASSESSMENT", "CORE OFFERINGS") — preserve as-is.
- **Hierarchy:** Title ~25pt inherited, Section headers 13-26pt BOLD, Body 8-11pt, Captions 7-8pt.

### Font Reality vs. Ideal
The templates contain legacy Inter, Calibri, Arial, and Noto Sans typeface references
inherited from Google Slides export. These are in static/decorative shapes NOT edited by
the skill. The skill MUST NOT introduce new non-DM-Sans fonts during editing.

**Pre-existing template fonts (DO NOT attempt to fix — not worth the XML risk):**
- Inter: Google Font ✓ — renders correctly in Google Slides
- Calibri: Microsoft font ⚠ — Google Slides substitutes with Arial (~2-3% wider metrics)
- Noto Sans: Google Font ✓ — renders correctly
- Arial: System font ✓ — renders correctly

**Calibri overflow risk on Google Slides:** Slide 13 strength bullets (35ch max) and
Slide 14 capability names are fitted to Calibri metrics. When viewed in Google Slides
(Arial substitution), text may overflow by 1-2 characters. Account for this by using
33ch max on edited text that shares a shape with Calibri content.

## 8. Headline System

### Rule
Every editable slide must have an insight headline — never a label. Headlines must contain a number, an entity name, and a verb.

### 4 Patterns
| Pattern | Structure | When |
|---|---|---|
| Paradox | "[Actor] excels at [X], yet [Y] threatens [consequence]" | Strength + weakness coexist |
| Quantified Impact | "[Situation] = [specific impact]" | Dollar/time saving available |
| Gap → Outcome | "[N] capabilities close [X]%, unlocking [outcome]" | Recommendation slides |
| The Window | "[Actor] positioned to [X] by [date] if [constraint] addressed" | Urgency/close slides |

### 9-Point Scoring
| Category | Points | Criteria |
|---|---|---|
| Specificity | 3 | Number present (+1), entity named (+1), verb present (+1) |
| Arguability | 2 | Could someone disagree? (+1), Not a tautology? (+1) |
| Source defensibility | 2 | Traceable to source? (+1), No inventions? (+1) |
| Narrative fit | 2 | Advances story? (+1), Connects to adjacent headlines? (+1) |
| Clarity | 1 | Reads naturally? (+1) |

**Threshold: ≥ 7/9 = production-ready. < 7 → rewrite before XML editing.**

## 9. Content Synthesis Rules (Critical for Narrative Quality)

The DMA reports contain rich, evidence-backed data. The skill must SYNTHESIZE this into compelling slide content — not just extract labels.

### The "So What" Test
Every data point must answer: "Why does this matter to the client's business?"

| Raw Extraction (WRONG) | Synthesized Content (CORRECT) |
|---|---|
| Assets: $2.5B | $2.5B in assets, up from $1.8B in 2020 (39% growth), driven by SEG expansion into aerospace and defense — creating the scale that makes platform consolidation ROI-positive |
| Efficiency Ratio: 86.97% | 86.97% efficiency ratio — 12 points above the 75% target, with 7 fragmented service tools identified as the primary structural driver of excess cost |
| P2C2: 2.11 | Products & Channels (2.11 vs peer 2.75, -0.64 gap): No digital account opening, no self-service portal, no real-time product recommendations — directly limiting deposit acquisition from 700+ Select Employer Groups |
| Strong mobile app | 4.7-star mobile app (top 5% for CU size) — the digital channel foundation that makes personalized deposit campaigns viable without branch expansion |
| ROAA: 0.34% | ROAA of 0.34% (below peer ~0.75%) constraining reinvestment capacity — every basis point of efficiency improvement releases ~$250K annually for digital investment |

## 10. First-Call Audience Rules

This deck is for a FIRST MEETING. The audience has never seen a DMA before.

### Headline Style (Alliant Standard)
The Alliant deck is the gold standard. Match this tone:

**Cover (Slide 1):** "Your digital maturity blueprint: where [Client full name] stands today and the investments that accelerate [specific business outcome]"
- Second person ("your"), full name, forward-looking, professional, NO abbreviations, NO maturity scores

**Profile (Slide 6):** "[Key metric] + [growth descriptor] create a [foundation] for [digital outcome]"
**Assessment (Slide 9):** "[Client] scores X.XX/5 — [strongest] leads at X.XX, [opportunity area] represents the highest-value investment"
**Heatmap (Slide 14):** "[Best capability] leads at X.XX; [worst] at X.XX presents the highest-impact opportunity"
**Close (Slide 20):** "[Business outcome] in [timeframe]: phased [approach] starting with [quick win]" — NO maturity scores in headline

### No Internal Codes in Client-Facing Content
- **NEVER** use pillar codes (P1C1, P2C3) in slide content or headlines. These are internal DMA identifiers.
- Use full capability names: "Products & Channels" not "P2C2"
- In the slide plan (which the user reviews), codes are acceptable as reference. In the ACTUAL slide text, spell out.

### Spell Out Client Name
- Cover slide (Slide 1): ALWAYS use full client name. Never abbreviate on the cover.
- Slide 6 (Org Profile): Full name.
- Other slides: abbreviation acceptable AFTER first full use.

### Translate Maturity Scores to Business Outcomes
DMA maturity numbers (2.45, 3.15) mean nothing to a CFO or CEO at a first call.

| DMA Jargon (WRONG for headlines) | Business Translation (CORRECT) |
|---|---|
| "Advance from 2.45 to 3.15 maturity" | "Recapture $2M+ in annual efficiency and fund deposit growth" |
| "3.15 maturity" | "Top-quartile digital capability" |
| "Maturity score of 2.45" | "Developing digital capability with clear acceleration path" |
| "Close the 0.64 gap" | "Unlock the deposit acquisition channel" |
| "+0.7 maturity points" | "Measurable improvement in member experience and operational efficiency" |

**Scores ARE acceptable in:** Pillar summary tables, heatmap data, assessment narrative body text. Just NOT in headlines visible to executive audiences.

### Abbreviation Policy
| Always spell out | Acceptable as abbreviation |
|---|---|
| Client name (first use per slide) | After first full use on same slide |
| iPaaS → "integration platform" | CRM, AI, ML (widely understood) |
| MDM → "master data management" | CEO, CFO, CTO (titles) |
| DXP → "digital experience platform" | SEG (if defined earlier as "Select Employer Group") |
| SFMC → "Marketing Cloud" | — |

### Strength Bullets (Slide 13) Must Be Descriptive
Strength bullets describe WHAT makes the area strong — they are NOT score labels.

| WRONG (score label) | CORRECT (descriptive strength) |
|---|---|
| "Fraud & Risk Mgmt: 2.82 (+0.32)" | "Zero enforcement actions, Verafin ML" |
| "Tech Architecture: 2.82 (+0.32)" | "Triple cloud + 6-tool DevOps pipeline" |
| "Compliance: 2.78 (+0.03)" | "21-year compliance director, clean record" |
| "Service reach: 2.69 (+0.19)" | "7 channels spanning digital to in-branch" |

The score appears in the CIRCLE INDICATOR next to the bullet — don't repeat it in the text. Use the 35 characters to tell the reader WHY it's a strength.

## Gap → Opportunity Reframing Table

Every gap must be reframed as an investment opportunity. The table below shows banned phrasings and their required rewrites. This applies to ALL client-facing slides (especially Slides 7, 9, 13, 16).

| ❌ Banned Phrasing | ✅ Required Reframe |
|---|---|
| "No MDM" / "no CDM" | "Unified data foundation ready to activate via [solution]" |
| "No digital account opening" | "Digital onboarding is the next high-impact member experience investment" |
| "No self-service portal" | "Self-service capability represents an immediate experience gain" |
| "No unified member/client view" | "The data consolidation opportunity unlocks a complete 360 profile" |
| "Not yet achievable" | "Positioned for activation once [prerequisite] is in place" |
| "Accumulated without orchestration" | "[N]-tool breadth creates a strong consolidation and rationalization opportunity" |
| "AI can't work without a foundation" | "AI readiness accelerates once the data foundation is unified" |
| "Gaps slow results" | "Closing these gaps accelerates [specific outcome]" |
| "No [capability]" (any variant) | "[Capability] represents an immediate investment opportunity" |
| "Lack of" / "absence of" | "Opportunity to establish [capability]" |
| "ISS-001", "ISS-002", etc. | **Remove entirely** — internal reference codes never appear on client-facing slides |
| "subcaps" / "subcapabilities" | **Remove entirely** — use plain business language |

**Pattern:** `[Current state] positions [Client] to [outcome] through [action].`
