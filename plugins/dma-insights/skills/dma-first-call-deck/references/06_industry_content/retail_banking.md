## Slide 5: Industry Forces

**Headline:** Neobanks, fintechs, and rising expectations are eroding the advantages traditional banks built over decades

**Sub-vertical label:** RETAIL BANKING IN 2026

**Introduction:** Here is what the data says about where the industry is heading.

**Key Statistics:**

### Stat 1: $4.7T

- in annual bank revenue at risk of diversion to fintechs and digital competitors
- 79 million Americans already use neobanks, growing 35% year over year. Digital-native competitors open accounts in minutes, not days.

### Stat 2: 60%

- of banks still in early stages of digital transformation
- Only 26% of customers are satisfied with their banking experience. Meanwhile, 57% say they'd switch for a better digital one.

### Stat 3: 15k+

- branches closed since 2019 with no digital experience to replace them
- Core systems built in the 1960s still process the majority of transactions. 35% of banks are dissatisfied with their own core platforms, but only 25% are prioritizing modernization.

## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Legacy infrastructure and fragmented data are the real barriers to growth

**Pain Points:**

**"Customers expect a neobank experience. We're giving them a branch experience online."83% of customers expect seamless information across channels. Legacy systems built piecemeal over decades can't deliver it, and 30% of digital transformation initiatives fail to meet their objectives.**

**"Our payments infrastructure can't keep up."**
- Only 62% of banks offer real-time payments. Customers and businesses expect instant settlement, and every delay is a reason to move to a provider that already offers it.

**"Our customer data lives in systems that don't talk to each other."**
- Data siloed across core, CRM, and channel systems blocks the unified customer view that 83% of customers expect. AI initiatives stall without a foundation, and personalization stays generic.
