## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Operational inefficiency is the biggest threat to margin, retention, and growth

**Pain Points:**

**“Our claims are taking over a month to settle.”~32 days to settle claims, up from 24 days in 2024. Claims experience is the number one driver of policy retention.**

**“We know fraud is slipping through, we just can’t catch it fast enough.”3x higher detection rates with AI versus manual methods. AI triages the highest-risk cases before losses occur.**

**“Our policy, claims and billing data lives in completely different systems.”50 to 100+ disconnected systems is the reality for most mid-to-large carriers. Without a unified foundation, AI stalls and analytics initiatives start from scratch every time.**

**“Our competitors are already using AI in underwriting. We’re still evaluating.”77% of carriers already have AI in production for underwriting and claims, compounding their advantage.**
