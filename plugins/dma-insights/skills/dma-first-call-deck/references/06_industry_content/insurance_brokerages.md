## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Manual workflows and disconnected data are quietly eroding your margin.

**Pain Points:**

**“Producers spend too much time on admin.”~60% of producer time is spent on admin tasks, stalling growth and making relationships suffer.**

**“Every carrier submission is different.”Managing 15–40 carrier workflows is one of the largest drains on producer time and speed.**

**“Our client data lives everywhere.” ~5–10+ disconnected systems means AI can't work, cross-sell stays invisible, and every analytics initiative starts from scratch.**

**“Commission is leaking and we’re losing revenue.”2–5% of earned commissions go uncollected every year due to manual reconciliation.**
