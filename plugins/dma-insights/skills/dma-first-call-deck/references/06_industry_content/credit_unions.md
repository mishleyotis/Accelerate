## Slide 5: Industry Forces

**Headline:** Aging members, digital gaps, and rising threats are putting cooperative banking at a crossroads

**Sub-vertical label:** CREDIT UNIONS IN 2026

**Introduction:** Here is what the data says about where the industry is heading.

**Key Statistics:**

### Stat 1: $72T

- in generational wealth transfer at risk over the next decade
- The average credit union member is 53 years old. Baby boomers account for 50%+ of revenue, but that share is declining fast. Only 11% of Gen Z are credit union members. You can't grow on loyalty alone.

### Stat 2: <10%

- of credit union sales happen through digital channels
- Regional banks are at 30%+. Largest U.S. banks invest nearly 100x what credit unions spend on digital. Members now expect Amazon-level experiences, and 57% would switch for a better one.

### Stat 3: 892

- cyber incidents reported across credit unions in the last 9 months
- Credit unions are 300x more likely to be targeted than other businesses. A single ransomware event in 2024 disrupted 60+ institutions through one vendor. Average breach cost runs $6-8M.

## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Legacy systems and siloed data are quietly eroding your member relationships

**Pain Points:**

**"Our members are younger than we think, but our digital experience isn't."75% of credit unions operate on legacy loan origination systems without true automation. Digital account opening takes 2x longer than competitors, and 30% of Gen Z members say they'd leave for a better digital experience.**

**"Lending is our lifeblood, but our process is bleeding borrowers."Lending makes up 84%+ of credit union income, yet origination volume has dropped 31% since 2022. Manual underwriting is rejecting qualified applicants. One institution found 9,000 missed loans worth $9M+ in interest.**

**"Our member data lives in silos we can't connect."Data is spread across core, LOS, CRM, and other systems with no unified member view. AI can't work without a foundation. Cross-sell stays invisible, and every analytics initiative starts from scratch.**
