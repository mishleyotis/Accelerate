## Slide 5: Industry Forces

**Headline:** Falling income, rising debt, and a generational transfer are rewriting the risk profile of agricultural lending

**Sub-vertical label:** AGRICULTURAL LENDING IN 2026

**Introduction:** Here is what the data says about where the industry is heading.

**Key Statistics:**

### Stat 1: $153B

- in projected net farm income for 2026, down 24% from the 2022 record
- USDA revised 2025 income downward by $25 billion in February 2026. Outside of cattle, crop producers face their third consecutive year of compressing margins.

### Stat 2: $625B

- in total farm sector debt, up 5.2% year over year with non- real estate debt up 35% since 2020
- Working capital is forecast to decline 9.2% in 2026. Borrowers are using government payments to service existing debt rather than reinvest.

### Stat 3: 58

- average age of U.S. farm operators, with 70% of farmland changing hands in the next 20 years
- 34% of operators are over 65. Fewer than 30% of farm families have a formal succession plan. The lenders who engage next-generation farmers now will hold the relationships for decades.

## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Manual credit processes and disconnected data are limiting your ability to manage risk in a tightening cycle

**Pain Points:**

**"Our credit analysis takes days when the borrower needs an answer now."**
- Farm financial spreading requires 4-8 hours per credit using manual methods. Standard commercial tools don't support Schedule F, cash-to-accrual adjustments, or FFSC ratios.

**"Our borrowers farm with precision technology but apply for loans on paper."**
- Only 58% of community banks use or plan to use e-signatures for ag real estate loans. Younger operators expect digital-first lending, and 70% of farmland is about to change hands.

**"We saw the commodity downturn coming but couldn't quantify the portfolio impact fast enough."**
- Corn prices dropped 34% from 2022 to 2025. FCS nonperforming assets rose 56% in a single year, yet most lenders still lack automated commodity stress testing.

**"Our farm operation data is scattered across systems that can't model a multi-entity relationship."**
- The average commercial farm operates through 2-3 legal entities. Without a unified view across entities, facilities, commodities, and crop insurance, early warning signals stay invisible.
