## Slide 5: Industry Forces

**Headline:** Revenue is recovering, but digital-native competitors, AI, and operational drag are rewriting who wins

**Sub-vertical label:** CORPORATE & INVESTMENT BANKING IN 2026

**Introduction:** Here is what the data says about where the industry is heading.

**Key Statistics:**

### Stat 1: $94B

- in origination and advisory revenue in 2024, up 32% year over year, while corporate banking revenue declined 2%
- The recovery is real but uneven. CIBs that modernize operations and reduce cost-to-serve will capture disproportionate value in a bifurcated market.

### Stat 2: 70%

- of banking institutions already using agentic AI through deployments or active pilots
- AI spend in financial services is projected to reach $100B by 2027. McKinsey estimates CIB operations could see up to 40% productivity gains through agentic AI adoption.

### Stat 3: 65%

- of financial institutions lost clients due to slow onboarding in 2025, the highest rate ever recorded
- Corporate KYC reviews take 61-150 days at over half of institutions. Average annual KYC/AML spend has reached $72.9M per firm, and it is still mostly manual.

## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Fragmented coverage, manual operations, and slow onboarding are costing you deals, clients, and margin

**Pain Points:**

**"Our coverage officers spend more time compiling data than covering clients."**
- Coverage officers spend 40-60% of their time on admin across 5-10+ disconnected systems. The top five US banks grew O&A revenue 33% by leveraging integrated client platforms.

**"We're losing institutional clients before we finish onboarding them."**
- 52% of institutions spend 61-150 days on a single KYC review. AI use in KYC jumped from 42% to 82% in one year, and institutions not adopting are falling further behind.

**"Our treasury and payments operations can't match what corporate clients expect."**
- Cash management revenue declined 2% to $198B as margin compression and fintech competition intensify. Manual payment investigations still take 3-5 business days to resolve.

**"Our institutional client data is scattered across systems that don't connect."**
- Data sits in 8-15+ siloed systems at most CIBs. Without a unified institutional relationship view, AI initiatives stall, cross-product opportunities stay invisible, and regulatory reporting is inconsistent.
