## Slide 5: Industry Forces

**Headline:** Alternative lenders, AI, and a refinancing wave are reshaping who wins in commercial lending

**Sub-vertical label:** COMMERCIAL LENDING IN 2026

**Introduction:** Here is what the data says about where the industry is heading.

**Key Statistics:**

### Stat 1: $936B

- in commercial real estate mortgages maturing in 2026
- The largest refinancing wave since 2007 is underway. Lenders with modern origination will capture a disproportionate share. Those still running 30-60 day cycles will watch it pass.

### Stat 2: 37%

- of non-agency loan closings now go to alternative lenders, surpassing banks at 31%
- Debt funds and fintechs are winning on speed, flexible structures, and cash-flow-based underwriting. This is a structural shift, not a cyclical one.

### Stat 3: 40-70%

- reduction in underwriting time for institutions using AI-powered credit decisioning
- AI models analyze up to 10,000 data points per borrower versus 50-100 in traditional scoring. 85% of banks globally already use AI in lending. The gap is compounding.

## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Manual processes and fragmented systems are costing you deals, margin, and talent capacity

**Pain Points:**

**"Our origination takes weeks. Our competitors take days."**
- 75% of institutions still operate on legacy loan origination systems without true automation. 81% of small businesses report difficulty accessing affordable commercial loans from traditional sources due to process friction.

**"Our underwriters are buried in spreadsheets instead of making decisions."**
- Manual financial spreading takes 2-4 hours per borrower and is highly error-prone. AI-powered platforms enable 3-4x more applications processed with the same staff.

**"Our RMs spend more time on admin than on relationships."**
- Relationship managers spend 40-60% of their time on administrative origination tasks. Without unified tools, each RM can effectively manage 40-60 relationships versus 80-120+ with AI augmentation.

**"Our borrower data is spread across systems that don't connect."**
- Data sits in 5-12 siloed systems at the typical commercial lender. No unified borrower view means AI initiatives stall, early warning signals get missed, and every analytics project starts from scratch.
