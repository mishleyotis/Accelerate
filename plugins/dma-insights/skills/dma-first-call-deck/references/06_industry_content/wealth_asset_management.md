## Slide 5: Industry Forces

**Headline:** Record AUM is masking a profitability crisis that only technology and operating model transformation can solve

**Sub-vertical label:** ASSET MANAGEMENT  IN 2026

**Introduction:** Here is what the data says about where the industry is heading.

**Key Statistics:**

### Stat 1: $128T

- in global AUM, a record, yet profit per AUM has fallen 19% since 2018
- Revenue grew 22% in 2024, but fee compression on new flows averages 40 basis points below existing AUM fees. 89% of asset managers report margin erosion over the past five years.

### Stat 2: $337B

- in active mutual fund outflows in North America in 2025 alone
- Passive AUM is projected to reach $70 trillion by 2030. Active managers must prove alpha or dramatically lower their cost to serve.

### Stat 3: 25-40%

- of total cost base recoverable through AI-enabled workflow transformation
- 80% of technology budgets still go to running the business, not changing it. Firms that flip that ratio are cutting product development cycles from 12 months to 3-4.

## Slide 7: Pain Points

**Question:** Do these pain points sound familiar?

**Subtitle:** Manual operations and fragmented data are eroding the margins you need to compete

**Pain Points:**

**"Our cost-to-income ratio won't budge."**
- The industry average sits at 68%, with most technology spend consumed by keeping the lights on.
- T+1 settlement has exposed how fragile manual post-trade processes really are.

**"Our investors expect digital self-service we can't deliver at scale."**
- 69% of institutional investors factor technology capabilities into allocation decisions. As retailization of alternatives accelerates, manual investor servicing cannot scale to meet the volume.

**"We're losing AUM to passive and can't out-distribute the outflows."**
- Active funds lost $337B in North American outflows in 2024. Distribution teams spend 40-60% of their capacity on manual RFP and DDQ processes instead of building relationships.

**"We lack the basic data processes to make AI work."**
- About half of asset managers lack processes to clean, normalize, and tag their own data. 66% of early GenAI adopters say they wish they had invested in data strategy first.
