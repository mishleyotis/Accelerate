### CG-52 · prose a customer is served names no pipeline tool

The customer audience is served the heatmap — the grid, the cell syntheses in
the evidence drawer, the evidence index, the focus areas — and every served
section's `narrative_thread` and `empty_state`. The serve layer's
pipeline-vocabulary net (`packages/shared/internal_ids.PIPELINE_TERMS`, read by
the api and by this gate) deletes the **whole field** for the customer when it
names one of: `NOT_RUN` inside prose, Clay, Explorium, Exa, Tavily, Firecrawl,
connector credit, RRF, `k=60`, engine v2, hot band.

Measured 2026-10-07 across eight promoted clients: 118 of 4,341 cell syntheses
served EMPTY to the customer — 36 of 216 on one client — because a producer
wrote "An Exa search found…" into the drawer. The build owner reported it as
"the customer view lacks heatmap details".

**Repair:** rewrite the sentence in the client's terms. Say what was searched
and what it established ("a search of the bank's newsroom, filings and trade
press found no…"), never which tool ran it. A status such as `NOT_RUN` becomes
"not assessed in this run", with the reason. The tool name belongs in
`sources_searched` or `r_layer`, which no customer is served.

**Not refused:** sections and pages withheld from the customer (Context,
alerts, evidence age, cohort patterns, starters, thought leadership, ceilings,
evidence coverage); keys no customer is served (`sources_searched`,
`queries_run`, `provenance`, `tier`, `ers`, `recency_band`, `r_layer`, …);
name keys (a CFO called Clay is a person); a registered source's own name
(`source_name`, `publisher` — the evidence store's, not yours); verbatim
fields (excerpts, quotes, urls, headlines — a quote is never rewritten); paths marked
`internal_only`; and
`NOT_RUN` on `heatmap.safeguard_gates`, which renders it by design.
