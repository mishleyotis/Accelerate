## O6 · Top findings

- **Section** `overview.findings` — **renders on** D1 (Overview)
- **Contract** Ranked findings, each with what, why and so what, a named consequence, a rejected alternative, and an inline expansion.

### Must present

Four to six findings, each a capability-named headline with a What/Why/So-what body and evidence chips.

The score chip and the anchor id must name the SAME cell. A subcap-grain score under a category id read '3.5/5' against a cell serving 2.77 on 59 clients.

The title must be a capability, not an evidence sentence, not a person's name, not a raw code, and not the body restated.

Bodies end in terminal punctuation and never repeat a sentence.

### The ranking basis agrees with the scores (RC-12(h), D-29)

`ranking_basis: impact_fallback` says no alignment score drove the order. If any finding
carries a non-null `strategic_alignment.score`, either rank by the scores (and say so) or
drop them — SWBC shipped `impact_fallback` with F-1 and F-5 at 0.8 ranked below findings
with no score. A count the narrative states ("five findings") equals the array; a finding
with no `platform_chips` beside others that carry them names its platform or states none.
Each `strategic_alignment.statement` and `so_what` keeps to its word band, and a recency
shown on a finding's evidence is the store's, copied, never re-derived.

### Information sources
