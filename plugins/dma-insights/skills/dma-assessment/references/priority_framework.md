# Improvement Priority Framework

Read this file during Phase 5 when ranking improvement opportunities.

---

## Six-Factor Priority Formula

```
Priority = (0.25 × BI) + (0.20 × RE) + (0.20 × CG) + (0.15 × EI) + (0.10 × QW) + (0.10 × TM)
```

| Factor | Weight | What It Measures |
|--------|--------|-----------------|
| BI | 25% | Business Impact — effect on core business outcomes |
| RE | 20% | Risk Exposure — regulatory/operational risk if not addressed |
| CG | 20% | Competitive Gap — distance from peer median |
| EI | 15% | Effort Inverse — ease of implementation (higher = easier) |
| QW | 10% | Quick Win — potential for early visible results |
| TM | 10% | Trend Momentum — whether situation is improving or worsening |

Each factor is scored 1-10. Priority Score ranges from 1.0 to 10.0.

---

## Factor Scoring Rubrics

### BI: Business Impact (Weight: 25%)

| Score | Criteria | Examples |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 9-10 | Direct revenue/growth impact; core to value proposition | P2C2 for digital-first strategy; P2C3 for service differentiator |
| 7-8 | Significant operational or cost impact | P3C1 automation for efficiency; P4C2 for decision quality |
| 4-6 | Moderate impact; supporting capability | P1C3 innovation long-term; P1C4 culture enablement |
| 1-3 | Limited direct business impact | P1C5 ESG (unless core to brand); nice-to-have improvements |

Evidence required: Revenue attribution, cost/benefit analysis, efficiency metrics, or logical linkage to outcomes.

### RE: Risk Exposure (Weight: 20%)

| Score | Criteria | Examples |
|-------|----------|---------|
| 9-10 | Active regulatory scrutiny or material compliance exposure | Open enforcement; recent MRA; pattern of findings |
| 7-8 | Elevated risk; recent issues in related area | Peer had enforcement; industry under scrutiny; near-miss |
| 4-6 | Moderate risk; standard controls in place | Normal operational risk; no exceptional exposure |
| 1-3 | Low risk; well-controlled area | Clean audit history; no regulatory interest |

Evidence required: Regulatory documentation (T1), industry trends, peer enforcement actions.

### CG: Competitive Gap (Weight: 20%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | >0.75 points below peer median; competitive disadvantage evident |
| 7-8 | 0.50-0.75 below peer median; meaningful improvement opportunity |
| 5-6 | 0.25-0.50 below peer median; room to strengthen |
| 3-4 | Within ±0.25 of peer median; at parity, not differentiating |
| 1-2 | Above peer median; competitive strength, lower investment priority |

Evidence required: Calculated peer benchmark data (not estimated).

### EI: Effort Inverse (Weight: 15%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Minimal effort — configuration change, policy update, vendor toggle |
| 7-8 | Low effort — small project, existing vendor can deliver, <3 months |
| 5-6 | Moderate effort — cross-functional project, 3-6 months |
| 3-4 | High effort — major initiative, new vendor, 6-12 months |
| 1-2 | Very high effort — multi-year transformation, core replacement |

Note: This is INVERSE scoring — easier = higher score. This ensures quick, impactful
improvements surface higher in priority ranking.

### QW: Quick Win Potential (Weight: 10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Visible improvement within 30 days; executive-visible results |
| 7-8 | Visible improvement within 90 days; measurable metric change |
| 5-6 | Results visible within 6 months |
| 3-4 | Results visible within 12 months |
| 1-2 | Results not visible for 12+ months; foundational investment |

Evidence required: Peer implementation timelines, vendor case studies, or logical inference.

### TM: Trend Momentum (Weight: 10%)

| Score | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| 9-10 | Declining trend AND below median; situation worsening |
| 7-8 | Stagnant for 2+ periods; no progress despite attention |
| 5-6 | Stable trend; not improving but not declining |
| 3-4 | Improving but still below median; progress underway |
| 1-2 | Improving and approaching/at median; on track |

Evidence required: Year-over-year comparisons, historical data, trend indicators.

---

## Priority Tier Classification

| Tier | Score Range | Label | Action |
|------|-----------|-------|--------|
| 🔴 CRITICAL | 8.0 - 10.0 | Requires immediate leadership attention | Address in current planning cycle |
| 🟠 HIGH | 6.0 - 7.9 | Important, near-term action needed | Include in next budget cycle |
| 🟡 MEDIUM | 4.0 - 5.9 | Valuable but can be sequenced | Medium-term roadmap |
| 🟢 LOW | 1.0 - 3.9 | Monitor; address as resources permit | Reassess if conditions change |

## Priority Calculation Example

```
CATEGORY: P2C3 Omnichannel Servicing
Current Score: 2.50 | Peer Median: 3.20 | Gap: -0.70

BI (Business Impact): 8
  Rationale: At 650,000 members, the 68% FCR and 8.2 min AHT translate to
  ~$1.8-2.4M in excess operating cost (Annual Report, T2; Internal Dashboard, T4)

RE (Risk Exposure): 6
  Rationale: CFPB complaints +18% YoY in service category (CFPB, T1).
  No enforcement, but trending in problematic direction.

CG (Competitive Gap): 8
  Rationale: -0.70 vs peer median. Largest P2 gap. App rating 3.4 vs 4.1 median.

EI (Effort Inverse): 5
  Rationale: Requires unified omnichannel platform — 6-9 month implementation.
  Existing contact center upgrade partially addresses.

QW (Quick Win): 6
  Rationale: Self-service quick wins (FAQ bot, mobile bill pay) can show results
  within 90 days while platform work proceeds.

TM (Trend Momentum): 7
  Rationale: Contact center platform upgraded 18 months ago but metrics flat.
  Investment made but outcomes not following.

PRIORITY SCORE = (0.25 × 8) + (0.20 × 6) + (0.20 × 8) + (0.15 × 5) + (0.10 × 6) + (0.10 × 7)
              = 2.00 + 1.20 + 1.60 + 0.75 + 0.60 + 0.70
              = 6.85 → 🟠 HIGH

VALIDATION: 2.00 + 1.20 + 1.60 + 0.75 + 0.60 + 0.70 = 6.85 ✓
Each factor is justified with specific evidence citations.
```

## Gap Register Format

For the Section 8 Gap-to-Solution Mapping table:

| Gap ID | Capability | Current | Target | Gap | Root Cause | Priority Score |
|--------|-----------|---------|--------|-----|-----------|---------------|
| GAP-01 | [P#C# Name] | [X.X] | [X.X] | [X.X] | [Institution-specific] | [X.XX] - [CRITICAL/HIGH/MEDIUM] |

**Target score** = min(peer_median + 0.25, evidence_ceiling). Never set a target above what
evidence could support.

---

## Presenting Priorities

When presenting improvement priorities:

1. Show the full 6-factor breakdown (not just the final score)
2. Cite evidence for each factor score
3. Rank all below-median categories by priority score
4. Group by priority tier for executive consumption
5. Note any categories where a small score change would shift the tier
6. Connect priority ranking to the sequencing in the transformation roadmap (Section 10)
