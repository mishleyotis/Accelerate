## How the columns were measured

| Col | Method |
|---|---|
| L-02 | `hard` = lines matching `\b(must|never|always|refuse[sd]?|mandatory|do not|don't|must not)\b`; `w/why` = those lines also matching `\b(because|so that|so the|so you|which is (how|why|what)|is how|is why|otherwise|measured|costs?|that is why|the reason)\b`. Reported as `hard / w-reason (%)`. Note: this counts a reason *on the same line*; the plugin's house style often puts the reason in the following sentence, so treat the % as a floor, not the truth. |
| L-03 | `neg` = `\b(do not|don't|never|must not|no longer|not optional|forbidden|banned)\b`; `pos` = `\b(must|always|mandatory|required|read first|first command)\b`. Ratio neg:pos; **flag > 1.0**. |
| L-04 | ALL-CAPS words of 4+ letters (`\b[A-Z][A-Z_]{3,}\b`) after excluding ids/acronyms (P1C1, CG-15, JSON, CSV, XLSX, NCUA, OPS/CUST/DATA/INFRA, T1-T5, RS-/SG-/AG- families, E-/MEM-/AUD- ids, etc. — full list in `measure.py`). Divided by tokens/1000; **flag > 10**. |
