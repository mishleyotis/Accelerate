## What this means with no human in the session

The thirteen (fifteen) defects were each found by **a person generalising from a rendered
page**. The gates written in response encode the *string that was reported*, not the
*judgement that found it*. In attended operation that is fine: the next paraphrase is
caught by the same person and becomes the next gate. **Unattended, the generalising step
is gone.** The gate set converges on the finite list of wordings already seen and is
permanently blind to the next one — and the pipeline reports PASS while it happens, which
is precisely the failure mode PR #2 names in its own opening line.
